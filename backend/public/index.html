    How to play
    1. You're the pumpkin Pac-Man on a real map. In Arcade you run along the actual streets like a maze.
    2. 🏠🍬 houses are volunteers handing out candy. Get within 25 m and it's yours.
    3. 🍫 Full-size bar houses are power-ups: the ghosts turn purple and run. Eat one for −5 s.
    4. 👻 In Arcade, a ghost that catches you costs +10 s. In Walk mode they're harmless and just drift around for atmosphere. Most candy wins; time breaks ties.
    5. 🧭 Route lays Pac-dots along the shortest walk through every house.
    6. Walk · Ranked uses your real GPS and gets its own leaderboard. The server checks your speed, so no cars or teleporting.
